We are building an AI-powered marketing workspace.

The current product is based on the open-source Postiz project, but this is only our starting point—not our destination.

Our vision is to create a platform where businesses can plan, create, collaborate on, automate, publish, and analyze marketing content from a single workspace.

Initially, the platform will include social media publishing and scheduling.

Over time, we want to expand into:

- AI content generation
- AI-assisted campaign planning
- Team collaboration
- Approval workflows
- Multi-brand management
- Marketing knowledge management
- Analytics and reporting
- AI recommendations
- Workflow automation
- Client management for agencies
- Integrations with popular business tools
- AI agents capable of assisting marketing teams

We do not want to compete solely as another social media scheduler.

Our goal is to become the operating system for modern marketing teams.

The product should be easy enough for freelancers while remaining powerful enough for agencies and growing businesses.

Every future feature should move us closer to becoming the primary workspace where marketing teams spend their day.
